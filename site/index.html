A research scan on skin, ingredients, products, tools and hair. It reads studies and registries, labels how deeply each source was read, and says where the evidence stops. Not medical advice.

Bookmarks, status and compare picks live in this browser on this device only. Prices marked EST are guesses, not lookups. Sources are linked on every dossier; where I only saw a snippet or abstract, it says so.
